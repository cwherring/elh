# Spanish Bites

**Teacher:** [Clara Ogden](/teachers#clara-ogden)

## Spanish Language Learning (Academic Advantage)

Spanish Bites is an engaging introduction to Spanish language and culture designed for multi-aged learners. Students will develop basic conversational skills, learn essential vocabulary, and explore Hispanic cultures through interactive activities, songs, and games.

---

## The Parakeet Nest (Ages 6–9, Wednesdays 3:30 - 5PM)

**Language, Culture & Playful Bites**

A playful, sensory-rich introduction to the Spanish-speaking world. Students will learn greetings, numbers, colors, and family words while exploring culture through stories, songs, games, and simple 'bites' (snacks and easy-to-assemble foods).

- Activities: games, songs, storytelling, crafts, tasting experiences
- Builds early confidence in conversational Spanish
- Encourages cultural curiosity and joyful participation

## The Condor Ascent (Ages 14–18, Tuesdays 3:30 - 5PM)

**Foreign Language Foundations**

Designed to support both conversational growth and foundational skills in reading, writing, and grammar. Students build confidence with present tense conjugations, useful vocabulary, and cultural context.

- Topics: family, school, travel, and descriptions
- Activities: written practice, role-play, short presentations, and cultural studies
- Credit opportunity: With consistent practice and completed assignments, this course may be credit-worthy toward high school Spanish

## The Jaguar Circle (Ages 9–18, Friday 9AM - 12:30PM)

**Cultural Immersion & Creative Entrepreneurship**

A 3.5-hour weekly immersion blending language, culture, and entrepreneurship in a project-based environment. Students rotate through blocks combining language practice with hands-on creativity.

- Cultural Flavors & Bites: Assemble and taste traditional foods while learning ingredient vocabulary
- Kidpreneur Lab: Entrepreneurial skills in a bilingual setting (menu design, branding, presentation)
- Art & Industrial Inspirations: Hands-on cultural crafts and functional design projects
- Music & Movement: Rhythm, dance, and song in Spanish
- Immersion Projects: End-of-semester cultural showcase with food, crafts, and bilingual presentations

**Duration:** 3.5 hours

### Block 1 – Spain (Weeks 1–3)
- Food & Flavor: Tapas bites
- Art & Craft: Gaudí-inspired mosaic
- Kidpreneur Lab: Menu card design
- Music & Movement: Flamenco rhythm clapping
- Project: Mini showcase: 'Un viaje a España'

### Block 2 – Mexico (Weeks 4–6)
- Food & Flavor: Tacos sencillos
- Art & Craft: Papel picado or Aztec calendar craft
- Kidpreneur Lab: Label design
- Music & Movement: Traditional song
- Project: Market-style bilingual pitch

### Block 3 – Caribbean (Weeks 7–9)
- Food & Flavor: Cubano sandwich or tropical fruit cups
- Art & Craft: DIY percussion craft
- Kidpreneur Lab: Branding basics – logo design
- Music & Movement: Salsa dance intro
- Project: 'Caribbean Café' showcase

### Block 4 – South America (Weeks 10–12)
- Food & Flavor: Arepas with cheese or brigadeiros
- Art & Craft: Andean textile craft
- Kidpreneur Lab: Group bilingual flyer project
- Music & Movement: Cumbia rhythm & dance
- Project: End-of-semester Cultural Showcase
