# Grade 2

## Reading: Thursdays 9:30 – 10:50AM

**Teacher:** [Debby Herring](/teachers#debby-herring)

Students will be reading from different types of writing as well as different favorite and/or classic children's authors like Mo Willems, Arnold Lobel, Cynthia Rylant, and Marc Brown. We will be focusing on comprehension strategies as well as decoding skills. Second semester we will move into reading short chapter books like Magic Treehouse (maybe sooner if they are ready).

**Homework:** Students will be asked to read 20 – 30 minutes nightly and complete some comprehension activities.  
**Class fee:** $525/Class per student for the fall semester, which includes curriculum and a non-refundable deposit of $75. The remainder is payable in $225 increments at the 6-week quarters.

**Teacher:** [Debby Herring](/teachers#debby-herring)
