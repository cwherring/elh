# Grade 1

## Reading: Tuesdays 9:30 – 10:50AM

**Teacher:** [Debby Herring](/teachers#debby-herring)

Students will be taught phonics and sight words as strategies to learn to read. Students will decode (sound out words), memorize sight words and learn to read short decodable books and stories.

**Curriculum:** All About Reading 1 and various literature  
**Homework:** 30-45 minutes daily (reading and practicing phonics)  
**Class fee:** $525/Class per student for the fall semester, which includes curriculum and a non-refundable deposit of $75. The non-refundable deposit of $75 per class is required at enrollment to hold your student's place. This fee covers the supplies needed for the class. The remainder is payable in $225 increments at the 6-week quarters.

## Language Arts (Writing, Spelling, and Grammar): Tuesdays 11:00AM – 12:30PM

**Teacher:** [Debby Herring](/teachers#debby-herring)

Students will learn to write words and sentences and eventually stories about their drawings in their journals. They will have the opportunity to share their stories with their peers. Second semester students will begin to use their phonics skills to "book spell" (Correct spelling) of three-four letter short vowel words. Students will also learn the parts of a sentence, proper capitalization and punctuation as well as nouns, verbs, adjectives and more. Students will write stories, make a list, write invitations and thank you notes and other real world writing applications.

**Curriculum:** Evan-Moor Grammar and Punctuation 1, All About Spelling 1 (second semester)  
**Homework:** 30-45 minutes - Students will have journal writing assignments as well as occasional grammar homework.  
**Class fee:** $525/Class per student for the fall semester, which includes curriculum and a non-refundable deposit of $75. The non-refundable deposit of $75 per class is required at enrollment to hold your student's place. This fee covers the supplies needed for the class. The remainder is payable in $225 increments at the 6-week quarters.
