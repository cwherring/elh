# Grade 9-12

## 7-9th Grade Learning Nest 2025-2026: Tuesdays/Thursdays 9:00AM - 12:30PM

**Teacher:** [Crystal Schmidt](/teachers#crystal-schmidt)

**Requirements:** Only a personal laptop, such as a Chromebook, needs to be provided by the student.

### Math

I am experienced in teaching math through Algebra 2 and enjoy helping students grow in confidence and see the beauty of math. Our Learning Nest will focus on solidifying middle school concepts like fractions, decimals, percents, and basic geometry. In conjunction with Physical Science, we will study unit conversions and scientific notation. We will embark on building the foundational skills of algebra, including solving equations with one and two variables, graphing linear equations, working with exponents, and more. Instruction will be tailored to the needs of the students and will be taught using a customized curriculum. Students will be given copies of my selected text to use as a reference at home.

### Science

We will be studying Physical Science this year, and I especially look forward to hands-on labs related to engineering. We will be using Journey Homeschool's video lectures and note-taking aids, as well as Novare's Physical Science textbook, to study at home. During class, we will review content highlights, master vocabulary, consider current advancements and applications in the field, and complete labs.

### Social Studies and English Language Arts

We will integrate these two subjects for a rich and meaningful experience. Our study of history and ELA will be rooted in what I'm calling our "Family History Project", "Autobiography and Journaling Project", and "Diversity and Perspectives Project". Students will be given the Abeka text "America: Land I Love" as a spine text to study. Students will be instructed in grammar, mechanics, composition, AI use, and writer bias as these topics arise organically through our projects. Students will practice online etiquette and academic accountability as they log in to Google Classroom every week to post an answer to a discussion question and respond to their classmates.
