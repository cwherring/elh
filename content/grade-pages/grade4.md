## Saxon Math 4th Grade: Tuesdays/Thursdays 8:30 – 9:30AM

**Teacher:** [Kacy Tribble](/teachers#kacy-tribble)

Saxon Math 5/4 is a homeschool math curriculum designed for students who have a solid foundation in basic math skills and are ready to move on to more complex concepts. It covers a range of topics, including addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, place value, estimation, fractions, decimals, geometry, measurement, and word problems. The curriculum uses a "spiral" approach, meaning it introduces new concepts gradually and reinforces them through consistent review of previously learned material.

**Curriculum:** Saxon 5/4 homeschool 2nd edition  
**Homework:** Expect 1-3 hours of homework each week  
**Class fee:** $675/Class per student for the fall semester, which includes curriculum and a non-refundable deposit of $75. The non-refundable deposit of $75 per class is required at enrollment to hold your student's place. This fee covers the supplies needed for the class. The remainder is payable in $300 increments at the 6-week quarters.
