## Computer Literacy: Wednesday 1 – 2PM (Academic Advantage location)

**Teacher:** [Chris Herring](/teachers#chris-herring)

Computer Literacy is a dynamic course designed for students in grades 5–12 to develop essential digital skills for academic, personal, and professional success in a technology-driven world. This course introduces students to fundamental computer concepts, including hardware and software basics, operating systems, file management, and internet safety. Students will gain proficiency in productivity tools (e.g., word processing, spreadsheets, and presentations), basic coding concepts, digital citizenship, and data management. The course also covers emerging technologies, such as cloud computing, AI, and cybersecurity basics, to prepare students for real-world applications. Through hands-on projects, collaborative activities, and problem-solving tasks, students will build confidence in navigating and leveraging technology effectively.

**Homework:** You can expect 2-4 hours of homework per week.
