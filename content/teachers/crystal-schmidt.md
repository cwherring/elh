# Crystal Schmidt

![Crystal Schmidt](/images/crystal-schmidt.avif)

My name is Mrs. Crystal Schmidt. My husband and I exclusively homeschool and have already graduated our two daughters to go on to successful college studies. We have three boys still at home, the youngest of whom will be in the 8th grade this year. I graduated from Concordia University in Seward, NE in 2003. I studied K-12 Art Education and TESOL (Teaching English to Students of Other Languages). Over the past 20 years I have taught K-12 art, science, math, and English language arts in a myriad of homeschool co-ops. I also direct a Camp Invention site in conjunction with the National Inventors Hall of Fame for one week each summer. My present hobbies include volunteering as a CASA (Court Appointed Special Advocate) for youth in foster care, tinkering with plants in my yard, and taking nature walks. I hope to hear your stories and meet you all soon!
