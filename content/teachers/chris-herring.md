# Chris Herring

![Chris Herring](/images/chris-herring.png)

Chris Herring is an accomplished IT professional with over 30 years of experience in software development, systems architecture, and team leadership, now transitioning to inspire middle and high school students as an educator in computer literacy and introductory computer science. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Oklahoma Christian University (1991), with minors in Engineering Science and Mathematics, equipping him with a robust foundation to teach technical concepts in an accessible and engaging way.

Throughout his career, Chris has demonstrated a passion for teaching and mentoring. At The Hershey Company, where he served as an architect and Software Development Manager for over a decade, he designed and led training programs for the enterprise-wide, data-science based forecasting tool that he developed, eventually training others to deliver these sessions. His ability to break down complex systems—using techniques and technologies like data-science principals, Python, C#, Azure, and Snowflake—enabled diverse teams to effectively use critical tools. At Leapnet, Inc., he mentored quality assurance teams in new technologies, developing web-based knowledgebases that reduced support issues by empowering users. Across roles at Cimarex Energy, NORDAM, and others, Chris consistently trained and mentored colleagues, leading teams and fostering collaborative environments through clear communication and hands-on guidance.

Chris brings a commitment to lifelong learning and technical expertise to the classroom, acquiring certifications such as ITIL Foundations and Snowflake SnowPro Core. His experience with C#, Python and system design makes him uniquely qualified to teach computer literacy to middle and high school students and introductory computer science with Python to high schoolers, inspiring the next generation of technologists. As a homeschooling dad to two high school graduates, he has many years of experience working within a homeschooling environment with his wife of 34 years, Debby Herring.
