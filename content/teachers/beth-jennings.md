# Beth Jennings

![Beth Jennings](/images/beth-jennings.png)

Hello! I'm Beth Jennings, and I teach high-school math and science at Empower Learning Hub. I hold a B.S. in Physics with a minor in Mathematics from Abilene Christian University, along with additional graduate coursework in Medical Physics. I have a year of tutoring experience with Academic Advantage and taught small-group Algebra II and Conceptual Physics classes at our co-op last year.

I believe that learning flourishes in relationships built on trust and care, and I strive to create a classroom environment where students feel safe to explore new ideas and take intellectual risks. My goal is for my students to feel supported, inspired, and genuinely excited to engage with the beautiful, surprising world around us.
