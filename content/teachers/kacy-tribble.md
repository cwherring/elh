# Kacy Tribble

![Kacy Tribble](/images/kacy-tribble.png)

Kacy is a 20-year veteran homeschool mom and teacher who has taught multiple grades across several states and a variety venues. She loves learning, and has a driving passion for integrating Bible, history, literature, and writing. She is dedicated to teaching in a dynamic, interactive, memorable way to make learning fun and relevant. Her mission is to teach children to recognize, love and pursue goodness, truth and beauty in every area of life as a life-long learner with Scripture as the foundation of their life and world-view.
