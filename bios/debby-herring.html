Debby Herring

My name is Debby Herring.  I have been teaching for over 30 years. I homeschooled my two children, Caitlyn and Jordan, through graduation . I have been married to my husband, Chris for 34 years. During our years of homeschooling, I have taught all ages, including high school history and English. I have a degree in Early Childhood and Elementary Education from Oklahoma Christian University. I taught kindergarten, first and second grade in public school before having my children. I am passionate about helping students learn to read and write.
